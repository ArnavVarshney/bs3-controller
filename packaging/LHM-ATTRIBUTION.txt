LibreHardwareMonitor — bundled binary attribution
====================================================

The BS3 Controller Windows installer ships LibreHardwareMonitor
(portable build) alongside bs3-web.exe so CPU-temperature fan curves work
out of the box.

- Upstream: https://github.com/LibreHardwareMonitor/LibreHardwareMonitor
- License: Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0).
  Full text: https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/
  The exact bundled version is recorded in lhm/VERSION.txt at install time.
- Source for the bundled build is available from the upstream releases page
  above, per MPL-2.0 section 3.2.

LibreHardwareMonitor is an independent work by its authors and is NOT
covered by this repository's MIT license; the MIT license applies only to
the bs3-controller code. No LHM source is modified or embedded — the
unmodified upstream binaries are installed next to ours and communicate
with bs3-web only through LHM's own WMI provider at runtime.
